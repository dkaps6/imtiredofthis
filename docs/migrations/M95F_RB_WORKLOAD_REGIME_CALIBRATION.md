# M95F — RB Workload Regime Calibration

## Research question

M95E showed two things at the same time:

1. a deterministic `team rushes × RB-room share × player share` mean remains too compressed and damages ordinary workloads when forced upward; and
2. the separate 20+/25+ workload-state ranking signal is strong and replicated, but its class-balanced raw scores and beta-binomial distribution are badly over-broad.

M95F asks whether the latent workload-state signal can be converted into a useful **calibrated hurdle / mixture distribution** without replacing the M94C central carry estimate.

The central football interpretation is:

`M94C normal-workload expectation + calibrated probability of 20-24 state + calibrated probability of 25+ state = player workload distribution`

This is not another mean multiplier.

## Frozen inputs

Research-only inputs:

- M95B frozen RB/offense/defense feature trace: run `33357785600`
- M94C frozen football-only game environment / team rushing trace: run `33353485070`

No sportsbook fields are used. No production code is modified.

## Protocol

### Raw workload-state score

The M95E-selected class-balanced logistic family is frozen. M95F does not reopen the raw tail classifier family search.

Targets:

- `actual carries >= 20`
- `actual carries >= 25`

### Temporal calibration development

For each 2024 week beginning in Week 5, raw tail scores are generated by training only on:

- all available 2023 games; and
- 2024 games strictly before the scored week.

For architecture selection:

- temporal OOF scores from 2024 W5-12 train the probability calibrator;
- 2024 W13-18 is the architecture holdout.

Three pre-specified calibrator families are compared:

1. **Platt** — logit of the raw score only.
2. **Beta-style** — log(raw score) and -log(1 - raw score).
3. **Football-aware calibration** — raw-score logit plus a compact set of false-positive controls: prior expected carries/share, workhorse/starter status, recent carry/share trend, backfield usage/concentration, QB rushing drain, neutral rush tendency, opponent RB carries allowed, late-season flags, and missing-history indicator.

The calibrator is an ordinary logistic model with **no class weighting**, because its job is probability calibration rather than rare-event ranking.

The selected family for each target is the one with the best 2024 W13-18 Brier score, breaking ties by ECE and log loss.

### False-positive operating point

A probability threshold grid is evaluated only on the 2024 W13-18 holdout. The operating point is selected by F1 among thresholds that flag no more than three times the number of observed positives on the holdout when possible. This is explicitly intended to test whether the enormous M95E false-positive volume can be reduced without destroying recall.

The threshold is frozen before 2025.

### 2025 validation

After architecture selection:

- temporal OOF scores from all eligible 2024 weeks are used to refit the chosen calibration family;
- the raw tail model is refit on all 2023-2024 data;
- the resulting calibrated probabilities are evaluated once on untouched 2025.

The 25+ probability is constrained not to exceed the 20+ probability.

## Hurdle distribution

M94C remains the official central carry estimate.

The distribution has three states:

1. **normal:** fewer than 20 carries;
2. **high:** 20-24 carries;
3. **extreme:** 25+ carries.

State probabilities are:

- `P(extreme) = calibrated P(25+)`
- `P(high) = calibrated P(20+) - calibrated P(25+)`
- `P(normal) = 1 - calibrated P(20+)`

The normal state samples a zero-centered empirical pregame-prior residual around the M94C player baseline and is truncated below 20 carries. High and extreme states sample their actual historical carry distributions from development data. This intentionally puts realistic 20-24 and 25-30+ outcomes into the distribution **only through state probability**, rather than raising every RB mean.

For the 2024 holdout, state pools are estimated only from 2023 through 2024 W12. For 2025, they are estimated from all 2023-2024 data.

## Primary diagnostics

Probability quality:

- AUC
- Brier score
- log loss
- expected calibration error
- predicted mean probability vs actual event rate
- quintile calibration
- frozen-threshold precision / recall / F1 / false positives

Carry distribution:

- p50 / p75 / p90 / p95 coverage
- p90/p95 counts reaching 20+ and 25+
- actual 20+/25+ tail diagnostics
- mixture-mean MAE reported only as a diagnostic; it does **not** replace M94C automatically

False-positive controls:

- Weeks 17-18 / Week 18
- recent carry drop
- recent backfield-share drop
- stable workhorse slice
- top false-positive examples and remaining 25+ misses

## Advancement gate

M95F can advance only as a **distribution/regime component**, not as a replacement mean, if untouched 2025 shows:

- calibrated Brier score better than the raw class-balanced score for both 20+ and 25+ in both the 2024 holdout and 2025 validation;
- 2025 mean calibrated 20+ probability within 0.04 of the actual event rate;
- 2025 mean calibrated 25+ probability within 0.02 of the actual event rate;
- p90 coverage between 0.87 and 0.95;
- p95 coverage between 0.92 and 0.99; and
- p90 25+ workload flags no more than five times the actual 25+ count.

Even a passing result remains research-only and requires integration review. Production change is always `0` in M95F.
