### Target and allocation
- `actual_room_share = targets / actual_te_pool` when `actual_te_pool > 0`, otherwise 0.
- target = `clip(log(actual_room_share + EPS) - log(b0_te_room_share + EPS), -2, +2)`.
- prediction = clipped Ridge prediction in `[-1,+1]`.
- entitlement score = `log(b0_te_room_share + EPS) + predicted_residual`.
- within each team-game, softmax entitlement scores to candidate room shares.
- **candidate targets = b0_te_pool * candidate_room_share**.
- candidate receptions = candidate targets * `b0_rec_per_target`.
- candidate receiving yards = candidate targets * `b0_rec_yards_per_target`.

Thus team TE target mass is exactly the B0/production pool.
