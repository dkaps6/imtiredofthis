# Joint Opportunity + Player Entitlement V1 — Architecture Decision

## Decision

The next model family will stop predicting each player's final yardage as an approximately independent positional outcome.

The single architectural thesis is:

> **A player's projection should be generated by a shared game/team opportunity state, then a conserved player-entitlement allocation, then a separate player/matchup efficiency distribution.**

This is the highest-priority research path. Broad position-wide feature hunts, static player-error corrections, depth-rank remaps, and new RB tail overlays are paused until this architecture is tested.

This document does not change production. It fixes the direction and the causal layer for subsequent experiments.

## Why this is the decisive hypothesis

The strongest repeated evidence in the repository points to opportunity structure rather than another generic yardage feature:

- QB-WR shared pass-volume residual mechanism: 2025 Pearson `0.6973`, Spearman `0.6706`, same direction `73.2%`; independent 2024-2025 confirmation using WR reception error Pearson `0.5239`.
- WR-R5: among 133 qualifying WRs, dominant recurring mechanism was TARGETS for 72 players, versus YPR 16 and CATCH_RATE 1.
- M38 WR target hierarchy improved WR receiving-yard MAE in all six seasons 2020-2025 while preserving team WR target mass.
- TE-R2: pooled TE target-error absolute mass was `60.405%` TEAM_TE_POOL and `39.595%` INDIVIDUAL_ALLOCATION; in the highest-error quartile TEAM_TE_POOL rose to `72.073%`.
- TE-R3 materially improved team TE-pool MAE (`2.604965 -> 2.293122`) and player target MAE (`1.666440 -> 1.605626`) but worsened player receiving-yard MAE (`16.183617 -> 16.339728`) because the correction was positive in `99.48%` of games. Opportunity correction without player-differentiated efficiency is therefore insufficient.
- TE-R4 proved strict-prior participation is broadly available: prior-1 any-team `97.91%`, prior-1 same-team `96.59%`, prior-3 any-team `95.92%`, with zero same/future observations.
- RB-R1 showed carry error remains mixed between team-room volume and individual allocation. RB-R3's strongest last-game share signal failed within-player persistence, showing that simple recency is not enough.
- The RB role-order remap failed badly overall, so current depth rank cannot simply replace usage-derived allocation.
- M95T formally ended retrospective RB tail-candidate development; carry opportunity remains strongly related to rushing yards (Pearson `0.7893`, Spearman `0.7890`) but efficiency/environment remains unsolved.
- QB player-specific mechanism routers were weak, while shared team passing environment was strong. Fixed player-error identities are not the answer.

The repository therefore has evidence for a causal hierarchy and evidence against repeatedly adding independent end-stat corrections.

## V1 causal architecture

For every team-game and Monte Carlo iteration:

1. **Game state**
   - expected offensive plays / pace;
   - pass-run tendency;
   - score/game-script state;
   - weather/injury/context already legal in the canonical football pipeline;
   - opponent defensive environment.

2. **Shared team opportunity**
   - draw one team pass-attempt state and one team rushing-opportunity state;
   - QB attempts and all receiver target pools consume the same pass state;
   - QB/RB rushing opportunities consume the same team rushing state;
   - no player may independently create opportunity mass that does not exist at team level.

3. **Position-group opportunity**
   - allocate finite team targets across WR / TE / RB receiving groups using pregame team history, role-room state and opponent tendencies;
   - preserve the already-supported M38 WR hierarchy rather than replacing it with a generic position reset;
   - TE pool is modeled separately before individual TE allocation;
   - RB room carries are modeled separately before individual RB allocation.

4. **Individual player entitlement**
   - allocate each finite position/room pool with normalized player entitlement scores;
   - entitlement is a pregame structural state, not a fixed player correction;
   - legal inputs include strict-prior target/carry shares, strict-prior offensive participation, same-team history, roster/depth state, teammate competition, injury-created vacancy, rookie/new-team state and already-audited matchup context;
   - current depth rank is contextual evidence, not direct carry/target authority;
   - learned entitlement outputs must be normalized within team/position so opportunity mass is conserved exactly.

5. **Efficiency layer**
   - catches / catch rate, YPR, YPC and explosive variance are modeled separately from volume;
   - player historical efficiency is combined with opponent/matchup environment only with strict-prior information;
   - a volume correction may not automatically inflate efficiency;
   - NGS/tracking fields may enter only after their strict-prior source coverage has been independently qualified.

6. **Joint outcome conservation**
   - use the already-supported C2 completed-pass / receiving-yard conservation mechanism as the receiving distribution template;
   - QB passing yards and modeled receiving yards share the same completed-pass process plus an explicit residual receiver bucket;
   - RB rushing and receiving remain separate components but share the same game script / play-volume state;
   - all final player prop distributions come from `simulation_v2` Monte Carlo, not independent post-hoc Normal approximations.

## Position decisions

### QB

- Keep M89/M90 as the current football-only mean anchor while its 2026 component integrity is audited.
- No new broad QB mean feature hunt.
- Replace independent pass-volume behavior with the shared team pass state.
- Use C2-style joint receiving conservation for distribution shape.
- Player-specific historical reliability may be used only as uncertainty/reliability information if walk-forward persistence is proven; never as a leaked retrospective player correction.

### WR

- Keep M38 as the target-share hierarchy anchor.
- Stop simple last-game target-residual and snap-level hunts already rejected by WR-R6/R8.
- Model residual **entitlement around M38**, not final yards directly.
- Entitlement inputs: strict-prior target share history, participation/routes/snaps where qualified, current room competition/injuries/role transitions, shared pass state, opponent coverage/matchup context.
- Separate catch conversion and yard generation from target volume.

### TE

- TE pool first, then individual TE allocation.
- TE-R3's nearly universal positive correction is prohibited as a production pattern.
- TE-R4 strict-prior participation is eligible and becomes the primary individual-allocation source family.
- Individual TE entitlement must combine pool state with participation/role and competition; efficiency remains separate.

### RB

- Keep the selected P3 / M94C central opportunity architecture as the baseline where qualified.
- Do not reopen M95 tail-overlay development or static depth remapping.
- Build shared team rushing opportunity first, then backfield allocation.
- Individual allocation uses multi-game carry share, snap participation, same-team continuity, teammate competition, injury/vacancy, QB rush competition and role-transition state.
- Translate carries to yards through a separate player + offensive environment + opponent efficiency model; explosive tail comes from the simulated efficiency mixture, not a detached probability overlay.

## Entitlement model form

The first learned individual-allocation candidate for each room will be an **offset model**, not a replacement model:

- start from the currently validated baseline share (M38 for WR; current B0/room share for TE; current P3/STACK opportunity share for RB);
- model a walk-forward residual entitlement score from strict-prior contextual inputs;
- add the residual to the log baseline share;
- apply a team/position softmax so candidate shares sum exactly to 1.0 within the finite pool;
- no post-result coefficient/blend search.

A single regularized linear family is preferred for V1 to keep the mechanism inspectable. Any exact alpha, caps and feature list must be frozen in the position-specific implementation plan before results are visible.

## Full-stack scoreboard

Every candidate must be evaluated as an **individual player projection model**, not only by pooled MAE.

Required outputs by position and pooled where applicable:

- MAE, RMSE, bias, correlation;
- median / p75 / p90 absolute error;
- catastrophic miss rates at position-appropriate thresholds;
- player-volume / role tiers;
- repeated-player and temporal stability;
- opportunity vs efficiency decomposition;
- Monte Carlo CRPS / interval coverage where distributions are available;
- season-by-season and latest-era 2024-2025 results;
- exact conservation/integrity checks;
- sportsbook inputs = 0 during football-model construction.

A lower pooled MAE with materially worse individual tails or unstable player tiers is not an automatic win.

## Market scoreboard

Sportsbook data remains downstream only.

After a football candidate is frozen and generated:

- compare the model distribution to the posted prop line;
- remove vig from market prices where both sides are available;
- compare model `P(over line)` / `P(under line)` to market-implied probability;
- track Brier score, log loss, calibration, closing-line value and realized return under a frozen decision rule;
- treat large model-market gaps as audit triggers, never as automatic football corrections.

The ultimate claim "beats Vegas" is not earned until prospective/frozen market scoring demonstrates it.

## Stopping rule

Do not respond to a failed V1 by adding nearby thresholds, alternate blend strengths, player-specific hand tuning, market-informed corrections or another generic position-wide feature hunt.

If V1 fails, diagnose whether the failure is at one of exactly three layers:

1. team opportunity;
2. player entitlement;
3. per-opportunity efficiency.

The next experiment must target the failed layer with a materially new football source/mechanism.

## Execution order

1. finish and preserve the 2026 QB football-only component-integrity audit;
2. implement shared pass-volume + C2 conservation in the actual full-stack path;
3. build WR/TE entitlement candidates around M38 / TE pool using strict-prior participation and role context;
4. build RB room-opportunity + entitlement candidate around the selected P3/M94C workload baseline;
5. evaluate all positions with the individual-projection scorecard;
6. only after football qualification, run downstream sportsbook comparison and begin prospective edge tracking.

No additional broad migration sequence is authorized ahead of this architecture.
