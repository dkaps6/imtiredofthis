How a leg gets on

Alt legs pass nine gates: on the field 30%+ of snaps, a real role, the rung well under his projection, his own record at that rung, matchup, injury status and price. They also pass a check for teammates coming back or going out. Rushing unders pass six football screens: on the field, played the last two games, a stable role, a sensible gap to the line, a game script that fits, and a matchup that fits.

About the odds

Each ticket's odds are its legs' FanDuel prices multiplied together. FanDuel's own parlay quote is the real price, and legs from the same game can price differently. Stakes shown follow a $100 split ($40 / $25 / $20 / $10 / $5).

What it is and isn't

Every leg is likely on its own, and a missed leg is variance, not a guess. The alt-line method won in week 9 and lost in week 10. Rushing unders held up on this season and on 2024-25, at about +2% on the older seasons. Bet sizes should stay small.
