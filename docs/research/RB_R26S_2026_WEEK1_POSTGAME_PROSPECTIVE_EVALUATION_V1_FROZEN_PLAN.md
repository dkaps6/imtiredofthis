## 1. Purpose

R26S is the first postgame prospective evaluation of the immutable 2026 Week 1 RB receptions candidate sealed by R26Q and observed pregame by R26R.

The study answers one question:

> On actual 2026 Week 1 RB/FB receptions, did the sealed R26 candidate improve player-level accuracy versus the protected production baseline without using outcomes, sportsbook information, or post-seal information to change the candidate?

R26S is an evaluation only. It may authorize a later production-promotion review. It may not promote, tune, refit, regenerate, or activate the candidate.

## 4. Outcome authority and participation authority

R26S may ingest outcomes only after the frozen plan and implementation are committed and hash-locked.

### Reception outcome source
Use nflverse weekly player statistics through:

`nflreadpy.load_player_stats(seasons=[2026], summary_level="week")`

Filter strictly to regular-season Week 1. The outcome field is official weekly `receptions` after normalization.

### Participation source
Use nflverse snap counts through:

`nflreadpy.load_snap_counts(seasons=[2026])`

Filter strictly to regular-season Week 1. Offensive participation is defined by an official Week 1 offensive snap count greater than zero.

### Zero-reception rule
A sealed RB/FB with confirmed Week 1 offensive snaps > 0 but no weekly-stat reception row is assigned actual receptions = 0 after exact identity/team reconciliation.

A sealed RB/FB with zero offensive snaps, missing participation evidence, a postponed/cancelled game, or unresolved identity is **not silently assigned zero**. That row is classified separately as DNP/zero-snap/unresolved and excluded from the primary accuracy cohort.

This rule prevents inactive bench players from creating artificial model accuracy or error.

The three unchanged CIN rows are controls and are not included in the primary changed-player MAE gate.

## 7. Frozen metrics

For every evaluable sealed row calculate:

- `actual_receptions`
- `baseline_receptions_mean`
- `candidate_receptions_mean`
- `baseline_error = baseline_mean - actual`
- `candidate_error = candidate_mean - actual`
- `baseline_abs_error`
- `candidate_abs_error`
- `paired_improvement = baseline_abs_error - candidate_abs_error` (positive = candidate better)
- winner: candidate / baseline / tie
- sealed candidate-minus-baseline movement

### Primary accuracy metrics
On the primary changed-player cohort:
- baseline MAE
- candidate MAE
- absolute MAE delta = baseline MAE - candidate MAE
- relative MAE improvement percent
- baseline bias
- candidate bias
- median absolute error for each
- candidate wins / baseline wins / ties

### Team-cluster bootstrap
Because RBs within one team share a conserved opportunity pool, bootstrap whole team clusters rather than independent player rows.

Frozen bootstrap:
- 10,000 resamples
- seed 42
- sample primary-cohort teams with replacement
- include all evaluable primary-cohort RB/FB rows for each sampled team instance
- statistic: baseline MAE - candidate MAE
- report mean bootstrap improvement and 2.5/97.5 percentiles

### Mechanism-specific within-room share metric
R26 changes allocation within an RB/FB room while preserving the room entitlement pool. Therefore also evaluate allocation directly.

For each team with at least two evaluable primary RB/FB rows and actual evaluable RB/FB receptions sum > 0:
- actual player share = player actual receptions / evaluable team RB/FB actual receptions
- baseline projected share = baseline mean / evaluable team baseline mean sum
- candidate projected share = candidate mean / evaluable team candidate mean sum
- compute player-level absolute share error
- report pooled baseline share MAE and candidate share MAE

This is mechanism evidence, not a substitute for actual-reception MAE.

18. sportsbook values used as football inputs = 0;
19. no postgame candidate regeneration, R9 refit, tuning, role redistribution, or parameter change;
20. production parameters changed = false;
21. production promotion performed = false;
22. live-shadow production activation performed = false.

A scientific failure of any required accuracy gate after valid outcome ingestion is preserved as failure. It must not be repaired by changing the candidate, cohort threshold, or gate.

A mechanical/data-contract failure before valid scientific evidence exists may be repaired only through a documented, frozen, value-neutral compatibility repair. The scientific evaluator and thresholds remain unchanged.
