## 2. Permanent PRISM assignment

PRISM is an **individual-player projection model**.

The named player in his current football state is the scientific unit. The system must reason through:
- identity / what this player has shown he is;
- current health/role/starter state;
- teammate competition and finite opportunity;
- opportunity quality;
- current team/offense/QB/coaching context;
- opponent/environment transmission;
- market-specific translation;
- reliability/uncertainty for this named player.

Never optimize for “position MAE” as the goal. MAE/RMSE are downstream diagnostics generated by player-level misses and hits.
